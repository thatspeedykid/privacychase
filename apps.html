Auris

Privacy-first audio enhancement.

Your headphones, everywhere. Auris makes your headphones sound as good on Windows as they do on the manufacturer's app — with zero telemetry.

  • Headphone profiles — AutoEQ database, 1,400+ devices
  • Per-app audio profiles — Spotify, Discord, games
  • On-device noise suppression via DeepFilterNet 3
  • AI scene detection — auto-switches preset
  • Built-in privacy audit log — should always be empty
  • Zero network calls during normal operation
